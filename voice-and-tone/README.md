# Voice & Tone

How HomeTruth writes and speaks — across product UI, marketing and brand. This
folder is **scaffolded** — full guidance to be added.

## Brand purpose (anchor for all copy)

> HomeTruth revolutionises property management and homeownership — turning a
> complex, often stressful journey into a simpler, smarter, more profitable
> experience. We empower people with clarity and confidence by translating
> complex property information into clear, personalised insight.

## Voice (constant)

- **Clear** — plain language; no jargon, no estate-agent spin.
- **Confident** — we know property; we make the reader feel in control.
- **Warm** — a helpful assistant, not a corporation.
- **Honest** — the name is "HomeTruth"; straight talk is the point.

## Tone (flexes by context)

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Onboarding / waitlist | Welcoming, encouraging |
| Product UI & guidance | Calm, precise, instructional |
| Marketing | Confident, benefit-led |
| Errors & alerts | Reassuring, solution-focused |

## Phrases in use

- Tagline: **"Your personal property assistant"** (style-guide lock-up uses
  spaced caps: `YOUR PERSONAL PROPERTY ASSISTANT`).
- Positioning: "Your AI-powered personal property assistant."
- "Your Home. Your Terms. Powered by AI."
