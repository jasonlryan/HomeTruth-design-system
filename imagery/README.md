# Imagery

Photography and illustration direction for HomeTruth. This folder is
**scaffolded** — art direction notes and approved assets to be added.

## Direction (from the style guide)

Two established imagery threads:

1. **Wooden building blocks** — tactile, warm, real. Echoes the building-block
   mark and the idea of constructing a home/portfolio step by step.
2. **Interior-design features** — calm, aspirational home interiors that show
   the outcome HomeTruth helps people reach.

## Principles

- **Real over staged:** natural light, genuine spaces, uncluttered.
- **Warm and calm:** the brand turns a stressful journey into a confident one;
  imagery should feel reassuring, never cold or corporate.
- **Framing:** the building-block framing device sits on white or black
  backgrounds — **not over photography**.
- **People:** where people appear, they should look like real homeowners, not
  stock-photo "executives".
